ShaderBox Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short: ShaderBox is a local-first shader authoring tool and requires no account. The app itself does not collect personal information and contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Network requests occur only when you actively use optional AI assistance or ShaderToy browsing/import.

1. Information We Collect

ShaderBox does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or other personally identifiable information. The app does not collect, upload, or sell personal data, and it includes no third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, profiling, or crash-reporting SDKs.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and remains under your control:

  • Projects, node graphs, GLSL code, and render-pass configuration;
  • Textures, cube maps, audio, and other resources you import or generate;
  • App settings and, optionally, AI service URLs, model names, and API keys;
  • Exported screenshots, videos, and other files.

Deleting a project, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app removes the corresponding local data. If your operating system enables cloud backup of app data, that backup may be handled by the OS or cloud provider under its own privacy policy; you can disable it in your system settings.

3. Network Access and Third-Party Services

ShaderBox accesses the network only for the following optional features:

AI assistance

When you actively use the AI assistant, your prompts, relevant project or code context, and any render screenshot you choose to provide are sent to the AI service you configure (for example, DeepSeek, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or DashScope / DeepSeek / MiniMax through the ShaderBox proxy). Those services process the request under their own privacy policies. API keys are stored only on your device and are used solely to call the endpoint you choose. No AI request is sent unless you enable and use the feature.

ShaderToy browsing and import

When you browse or import ShaderToy creations, the app requests public content from shadertoy.com. Those requests are subject to ShaderToy's own privacy policy.

Other

Outside the features above, the app does not transmit data to other servers in the background.

4. Permissions

  • Network (INTERNET): used for the optional AI and ShaderToy features described above.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): used for haptic feedback during editing.
  • File access: import and export use the system file picker; the app accesses only the files or folders you explicitly select.

ShaderBox does not request location, camera, contacts, or microphone permissions.

5. Data Sharing

ShaderBox does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Request content is sent to the third-party services described above only as necessary to provide features you actively use. We do not otherwise disclose your data to third parties.

6. Retention and Deletion

ShaderBox does not store your projects or personal data on its own servers. You decide how long local data is retained: you can delete projects in the app, clear app data, or uninstall the app at any time. Retention of request content by third-party services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

ShaderBox is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Minors should use the app and its optional network features under the guidance of a guardian.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be noted in the project repository or app release notes.

9. Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about this privacy policy, please contact us via GitHub Issues.
